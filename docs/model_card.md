# Frozen FNO model card

The direct-horizon FNO takes an estimated field, zone temperatures, nominal material context and an entire candidate 12-step heater-power plan. It predicts 12 future field and zone states. Training uses 24 whole trajectories, validation uses six, and a separate six-trajectory calibration split is reserved. The architecture has width 24, four Fourier layers and eight modes. Three data-only and three physics-informed checkpoints were trained with seeds 101, 202 and 303. The physics-informed option adds a differentiable finite-volume residual with weight 0.01.

The held-out eight-trajectory oracle forecast test on a 32×32 grid found mean field RMSE 1.117 K for data-only FNO and 1.164 K for PINO. The ROM forecast comparator had 0.386 K. In 12 partially observed closed-loop scenarios with the same CEM optimizer, data-only FNO averaged 24.42 K field tracking RMSE, PINO 29.22 K and ROM-CEM 11.54 K. The PINO residual average was lower than the data-only residual, but this did not improve field forecasting or control. Three training seeds showed large closed-loop spread, including runs that selected almost no heating.

These checkpoints are research artifacts for reproducing the negative comparison and studying control-relevant surrogate errors. They are not validated control models for equipment. A change in zone count, geometry, material range, actuator model or sensor layout requires retraining and evaluation. Checkpoint metadata contains a SHA-256 for the numeric `.npz` weights; the loader disables Python pickle. Source and artifact hashes must be checked before use.
